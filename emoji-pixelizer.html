Emoji Pixelizer

Turn any emoji — or your own image files — into pixel-art sprites for the game. Tune the recipe once, preview at real card sizes, then download a game-ready PNG. Instruments go in sprites/instruments/, animals in sprites/animals/. Same pixelization as bake-emoji-sprites.py, just interactive.

How the sprite is used: the download is a tiny 40×40 PNG at the pixel grid's native size; the game upscales it nearest-neighbour (image-rendering:pixelated), so small is correct. Drop it in sprites/instruments/ or sprites/animals/ and set sprite:"instruments/<file>.png" (or a bare "<file>.png" for an animal) on that entry in critter-hunt.html.

Caveat: an emoji renders with your device's emoji font (Apple on a Mac), so the baked PNG freezes that look for every player — the tradeoff for cross-platform consistency.
