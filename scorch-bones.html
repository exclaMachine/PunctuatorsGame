🔥 Scorch the Bones

Morendo stalks the dark. Stay near-silent and he wanders, oblivious. Whisper to catch his eye — a ! appears and he comes for you. Let him get close, then go loud to erupt the fire and scorch him. A burned Morendo bolts into the dark — lure him back and scorch him again, round after round, until he crumbles. Blasting while he's far only scares him off — and every eruption spends Breath, so you can't just scream. Don't let your Torch go dark.

Use your voice, a harmonica, a piano — anything. No need to shout; a quick loud note is plenty.
