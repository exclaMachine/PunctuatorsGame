M1 of the Scriptorium (docs/inklings-typography.md). Trace the letter stroke by stroke; the grade is per-stroke fidelity against the authored skeleton, and the ink is a nib stamped along your path — which is the whole claim of §3: stroke contrast is a record of the tool, so one skeleton plus a pen gives you a typeface. Glyphs come from Wordshape's authored alphabet (data/wordshape-alphabet.json if present, else sliced live out of wordshape-draw.html so the two can't drift).
