The Devil's Forge

"An alloy is a chord — several metals melted into one, just as several notes sound as one. Don't fumble at my anvil, smith. Program the forge, then run it clean. Forge me a weapon that rings — anything dissonant comes out brittle, and cracks."

Lay a tape of forge-glyphs (the program), Draft it to preview the blade, then ▶ Forge to run it once and strike-test. On-spec rings and cuts her; dissonant shatters and she strikes back. Fewer, cleaner glyphs forge a masterwork.

⚒ +M3 / +m3 — weld a major / minor 3rd on top. +M3 then +m3 = major; +m3 then +M3 = minor.
🔧 Temper ▲▼ — nudge the top tone a half-step (compose thirds, add leading tones).
🪨 Grind — invert: lift the lowest tone an octave (for inversion orders).
🧪 Etch — add a 7th color (turn a triad into a 7th chord).
🔥 Anneal — resolve the harshest dissonance — the antidote to her injected tritone flaw ⚡.
