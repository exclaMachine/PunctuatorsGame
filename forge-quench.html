The Devil's Forge

"An alloy is a chord — several metals melted into one, just as several notes sound as one. You want to shatter me? Then forge me a weapon that rings. Anything dissonant comes out brittle… and cracks."

Stack thirds onto the root ingot to build the ordered chord, then Quench to strike-test it. A clean, on-spec weapon rings and cuts her; a dissonant one shatters and she strikes back. Fewer, cleaner strikes forge a masterwork (more damage).

+M3 then +m3 = major · +m3 then +M3 = minor · Temper nudges the selected ingot a half-step (resolve her tritone flaws with it). Click an ingot to select it.
