M3 · THE WORD

Flick a direction and you strike — sliding until something stops you, swallowing every letter you cross.

Your body is the rack. Every segment behind your head is a slot — letters fill from the neck back, and the oldest rides your tail.

Hold more than seven and the oldest falls out of your tail. It lands as a block — and Apep eats it and climbs faster, for good.

Spell a word with what you hold and the letters leave cleanly, you score, and Apep is pushed back down. Nothing pauses while you do it.
