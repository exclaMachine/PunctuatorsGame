KAIMOJU · 声マッチ VOICE-MATCH PROBE

Offline, no server, no speech-to-text. It records the mic (Web Audio), turns each utterance into MFCC features (a mini-spectrogram of timbre over time), and compares it against templates you record in your own voice using DTW. That captures the actual phonetics — so it can tell pika from kira from doki, not just "a 2-beat pattern." Speaker-dependent: record each sound a few times first, then say it and watch it match.
