🐾 Animal Sound Lab

Tune how your collected Critter Hunt cries play as cards in Mujicians. Each clip is cropped to a note length and pitch-shifted (same Web-Audio path as Mujicians' renderSample). Pick an animal, choose Pitched or One-shot, shape it with the sliders, audition on the keyboard / scale / drum loop, then Save — your tuning is shared with Mujicians and never set in stone. Must be served over http (Live Server) — file:// can't decodeAudioData.
